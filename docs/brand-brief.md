# Creative brief — Birta wordmark & logo (first-draft concepts)

For concept artists producing **first-draft** logo/wordmark directions. Divergent and
rough is the goal this round, not polish. Self-contained; the full reasoning lives in
[`docs/BRAND.md`](BRAND.md) if you want it, but you shouldn't need it.

---

## What Birta is

**Birta Labs** is a small software studio. Its first product, **Birta Writer**, is a
WYSIWYG Markdown editor for VS Code — you edit the real `.md` file in place, formatted, and
what you see is what's on disk. More products will follow (a hypothetical *Birta Budget*,
etc.). The audience sits between developer tools and consumer software: technical, taste-
driven, allergic to hype.

**The name is the concept.** *Birta* is Icelandic for the **quality of light** — the
ambient glow in a room, not a lamp — and the verb **to publish / reveal / bring to light**
(*birting* = a publication, and also daybreak). That is the product in one word: the raw
source *brought into the light and shown plainly*. Don't invent a new concept; render this
one.

## The system you're designing into

The identity is **the word, drawn** — never a symbol standing in for it. Three tiers:

- **Birta** — one reusable wordmark, the constant across every product. Warm, humanist.
- **Birta [Product]** — each product word gets its **own distinctive typography**: *Writer*
  fluid and contemporary (think a thick, confident ink stroke); a future *Budget* would be
  structural and solid. The product word must be strong enough to **stand alone** with no
  "Birta" beside it.
- **Birta Labs** — the company name; rare, "Labs" set plainly. Not your focus this round.

## What to deliver, per concept

All **black and white**, vector, rough:

1. **The "Birta" wordmark** — the constant. Show it large, and shrunk small.
2. **"Writer" as a standalone wordmark** — fluid/contemporary; must hold on its own.
3. **One "Birta Writer" lockup** — the two together (this becomes app icons, so imagine it
   in a rough square).
4. **A utility glyph** — a single letterform pulled from the "Birta" wordmark, to work at
   favicon size (16 px). A functional device, *not* a monogram-as-logo.
5. **One sentence** naming the concept and how it ties to *birta*.

Optional bonus: a quick "Budget" in a structural voice, just to show the system flexes.

## Hard rules

**Do**
- Render **light through value and negative space** — how light falls, the space it fills —
  as a quiet detail *inside the letterforms* (e.g. a lit counter, a value split).
- Keep "Birta" **humanist and warm**. Product words may run cooler/more structured if the
  product calls for it.
- Make it **plain, confident, unfussy** — the plainer of two close options wins.
- Design so it survives **one colour** and **small sizes**.

**Don't**
- **No monogram** — "Birta" is never reduced to a single "b" or a symbol. (The utility
  glyph is a functional exception, not a logo.)
- **Don't depict a light source** — no sun, ray-burst, lightbulb, lens-flare, spark.
- **No literal props** — no pen-nibs, documents/pages, cursors.
- **No Nordic kitsch** — no runes, glaciers, aurora, flags. The warmth carries the origin.
- **No gradients, no drop shadows, bevels, or glass.** No colour this round (see below).
- **Not a cold geometric grotesk** for the "Birta" constant.

## Colour — don't worry about it

Work in **black and white only.** The final palette is already decided (inherited from
`harlanlewis.com`: warm neutral grounds, a magenta/orange accent that shifts light-to-dark).
Resolved wordmarks will drop into it later. If it doesn't work in one value, it isn't done.

## How drafts will be judged

- **Stands alone** — cover "Birta": does "Writer" still read as a distinctive mark?
- **Fits the square** — the lockup works as an app icon, two words, no symbol to lean on.
- **Down to the glyph** — the utility glyph is legible and unmistakable at 16 px, one
  colour, on light and dark.
- **One-sentence concept** — it ties back to *birta*.
- **Plain, warm, appropriate** — reads between dev-tool and consumer; not clinical, not
  cluttered.
- **Ownable** — distinctive enough to protect (the name is a common word; the *lettering*
  is the asset).

## Reference

- Typographic direction to react to (not copy): earlier wordmark sketches labelled
  *ARIA* (organic serif), *FLOW* (integrated ligature), *CURVE* (fluid humanist),
  *STRUCTURAL* (sculpted, variable weight).
- Tone/world: `harlanlewis.com`.
